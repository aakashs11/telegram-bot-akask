<decisions_made>

- Use Supabase PostgreSQL in Mumbai because it matches ADR-002 and the future
  Mem0/pgvector architecture.
- Keep persistence provider-neutral through `DATABASE_URL`, SQLAlchemy async,
  asyncpg, and Alembic; do not couple domain services to `supabase-py`.
- Use Supabase Free during migration while Sheets remains a recoverable copy;
  upgrade to Pro before PostgreSQL becomes the sole source of truth unless
  independently tested off-site backups are deliberately accepted.
- Use one event UUID across Sheets and PostgreSQL, idempotent inserts, and
  reconciliation because cross-system writes cannot be atomic.
- Keep logging fail-open so analytics failures never break student replies.
- Do not remove Sheets before the real observation and rollback windows pass.

</decisions_made>
